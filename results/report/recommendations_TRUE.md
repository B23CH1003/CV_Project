# Menu and quantity recommendations

- **Fish, Friday dinner - chronic** (mu 53.3%, R 1.00, TWCI 53.3). Cut the prepared quantity by about 48%, or swap it for a more popular dish.
- **Bread, Sunday breakfast only - weekly-recurring** (mu 54.4%, R 1.00, TWCI 54.4). Normal on other days, so reduce it by about 50% in this slot only, or move it to a slot where it is eaten.
- **Chicken, Wednesday dinner only - weekly-recurring** (mu 54.6%, R 1.00, TWCI 54.6). Normal on other days, so reduce it by about 50% in this slot only, or move it to a slot where it is eaten.
- **Salad with eggs - pairing effect** (42.1% with eggs vs 17.7% without (4 of 4 eggs slots raised)). Serve a smaller salad portion (or skip it) on eggs days.
- **Pasta - serving-time decay** (+19.2% after 60 min (+12.6%/hour)). Cook it in two batches or keep it hot, so plates served late in the service are fresh.
- **Rice - rising waste** (+6.1%/week (trimmed weekly means [11.3, 18.6, 24.3, 29.8])). Check the recipe and get student feedback now, before it becomes chronic.
