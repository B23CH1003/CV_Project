# Menu and quantity recommendations

- **Fish, Friday dinner - chronic** (mu 49.8%, R 1.00, TWCI 49.8). Cut the prepared quantity by about 45%, or swap it for a more popular dish.
- **Bread, Sunday breakfast only - weekly-recurring** (mu 54.2%, R 1.00, TWCI 54.2). Normal on other days, so reduce it by about 50% in this slot only, or move it to a slot where it is eaten.
- **Chicken, Wednesday dinner only - weekly-recurring** (mu 54.5%, R 1.00, TWCI 54.5). Normal on other days, so reduce it by about 50% in this slot only, or move it to a slot where it is eaten.
- **Soup, Thursday lunch only - weekly-recurring** (mu 31.5%, R 0.75, TWCI 23.6). Normal on other days, so reduce it by about 24% in this slot only, or move it to a slot where it is eaten.
- **Salad with eggs - pairing effect** (41.6% with eggs vs 17.8% without (4 of 4 eggs slots raised)). Serve a smaller salad portion (or skip it) on eggs days.
- **Pasta - serving-time decay** (+18.7% after 60 min (+12.3%/hour)). Cook it in two batches or keep it hot, so plates served late in the service are fresh.
- **Rice - rising waste** (+6.0%/week (trimmed weekly means [10.3, 17.2, 23.8, 28.0])). Check the recipe and get student feedback now, before it becomes chronic.
